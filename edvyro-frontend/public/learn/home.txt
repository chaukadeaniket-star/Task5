Quick statistics: Internships, partner companies, and students placed
Hero visual: Opportunity search panel with sample internship cards

/*{SEARCH AND FILTERS}*/
Search panel: Search internships, companies, and skills
Category filters: Filter the displayed internship cards
Popular search terms: React, Python, UI/UX, Java, and Internships
View All Internships action: Opens pages/Browse.html

/*{FEATURES AND MODALS}*/
Feature cards: Curated Opportunities, Build Real Skills, Track Your Progress, Career Growth
Search overlay: Opens from the navbar search control and can be closed with its close control, backdrop, or Escape
Video modal: Watch Video requires a signed-in student; visitors are redirected to login. The modal closes from its close control or backdrop.
Keyboard behavior: Search and modal controls support keyboard interaction

/*{RESPONSIVE LAYOUT}*/
Stylesheet breakpoints adjust navigation, hero content, cards, and modal layout for smaller screens.

/*{DEMO LIMITATIONS}*/
Internship counts and preview cards are static demonstration content.
This frontend does not connect to a live jobs or account service.
The shared page guard and video restriction are client-side demo behavior, not secure authorization.
