/*{PAGE OVERVIEW}*/
Page: Create Account
HTML: pages/register.html
Stylesheet: styles/login.css
Purpose: Create a demo student profile and sign the student into InternBoard.

/*{LAMP INTERACTION}*/
The registration form starts hidden.
Clicking the lamp reveals the form, updates the hint, and focuses the full-name field.

/*{REGISTRATION FORM}*/
Fields: Full name, email address, password, and confirm password.
Browser validation requires the name, a valid email, and passwords of at least 8 characters.
Password and confirmation must match.
The Show/Hide control toggles both password inputs.
The password is not saved.

/*{PROFILE AND NAVIGATION}*/
Successful submission saves the student's name, email, and generated Intern ID to localStorage under internboard.studentProfile.
The password is not saved. The student is returned to the protected page they originally requested, or to pages/student_profile.html by default.
The Login link opens pages/login.html.

/*{DEMO LIMITATIONS}*/
This is not a server-backed account. The profile remains in this browser until the student logs out or clears browser storage.
