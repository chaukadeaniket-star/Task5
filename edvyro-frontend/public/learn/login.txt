/*{PAGE OVERVIEW}*/
Page: Login
HTML: pages/login.html
Stylesheet: styles/login.css
Purpose: Present a lamp-reveal login form for returning InternBoard students.

/*{LAMP INTERACTION}*/
The login form starts hidden.
Clicking the lamp turns on the light, reveals the form, updates the hint, and focuses the email field.
Accessible pressed state and labels are updated when the lamp is activated.

/*{LOGIN FORM}*/
Fields: Email address and password.
Browser-required validation checks that both fields are entered.
The Show/Hide control toggles password visibility.
Submitting reuses a saved matching student profile or creates a demo profile from the entered email.
The profile is saved in localStorage under internboard.studentProfile.
After successful demo sign-in, the student returns to the protected page they originally requested, or opens pages/student_profile.html by default.

/*{NAVIGATION}*/
The brand link returns to pages/home.html.
Create an account opens pages/register.html and preserves the requested destination.
Browse internships opens pages/Browse.html when the visitor is signed in; otherwise the shared page guard redirects to login.

/*{DEMO LIMITATIONS}*/
This is not server-backed authentication and the password is not verified.
The browser-stored profile keeps the student signed in across pages and reloads until logout or browser storage is cleared.
